# SGCC authoring and HVDC tuning — 26 October 2026

SGCC is the aggregation exception in the [original research](2026-10-18-world-power-grids-top-8.md),
section A: North–Central, East, Northwest, Northeast and Southwest are five synchronous
components, not one machine. North and Central share the Changzhi–Nanyang AC connection:
[NEA, 30 June 2025, published 11 August 2025](https://www.nea.gov.cn/20250811/5f1ab46a1f3e4adf94751eb196b4e5df/c.html)
(rechecked 26 October 2026). The Southwest classification follows the SGCC-authored
[2025 technical paper](https://www.csee.org.cn/pic/u/cms/www/202505/09141914agtx.pdf)
already documented in the original research. Its PDF could not be re-fetched by the web reader
(size limit); the existing research remains its evidence.

The footprint is an administrative proxy, using Natural Earth v5.1.2 admin-1. Tibet is the
province-level proxy for connected Tibet; generator enclaves are unresolved, as in the original
research. Inner Mongolia is split at 118°E, a schematic electrical boundary that puts Hohhot
in North–Central and Chifeng in Northeast. It does not claim a prefecture-accurate boundary.
Yunnan and the Southern China area are excluded, as are Taiwan, Mongolia and foreign grids.
The operating footprint takes no position on territorial claims.

The seven geography sidecars are reproducible from `tools/region_authoring/selections/sgcc_*.args`.
Raw shared provincial borders are retained so independently simplified area boundaries cannot
introduce overlaps or gaps. Area `geography` references reuse the same bundle members as region
land. Small islands follow the existing two-cell cutoff. No generated geography is pasted into
hand-authored region data.

Economics reuse the national Chinese construction proxies and explicit pacing judgments from
[Plan 7 economics](2026-10-25-plan-7-regional-economics.md), rows P7-SC-001–006, with their US and
FX comparators. This is not new evidence of provincial price differences. The catalog comments
retain the original arithmetic. The Guangdong tariff proxy P7-SC-005 is retained as an openly
illustrative national game tariff; it is not described as a Beijing retail tariff. Cash remains
freely tuned. Economic zones are omitted by authoring choice: no supported provincial price
comparison was assembled.

| ID | Game judgment | Value and basis |
|---|---|---|
| P8-GAME-AC | AC construction | 444,000 CNY/km from Plan 7’s national coal-relative proxy × 26.95 km/cell = 11,966,000 CNY/cell rounded; 201.07 km/month ÷ 26.95 ≈ 7 cells/month |
| P8-GAME-DC | HVDC catalog | 1,000 MW rating; 400 million CNY combined converters; 12 million CNY/cell; 4 converter months; 50 MW/min ramp; 1% loss per converter plus 0.02% per cell; 600-month book life; 4,000 designed hours/year |

Every HVDC number is game tuning, not a sourced real project estimate. Long corridors cost and
consume construction time, DC has finite transfer capacity, and an idle link is penalised by the
spec’s utilisation review. No optional HVDC economic override is needed in SGCC, but the loader
and quote resolver support it for authored regions.

City coordinates identify real metros. Loads are illustrative pacing weights, initially scaled from
40,850 MW × 3,170/980 ≈ 132 GW, not observed city load; the starter revision below down-weights
its inherited territories. Beijing, Tianjin, Langfang and the
first expansion Baoding deliberately stay smaller to support a compact starting fleet.
Thermal sites near northern generating centres are schematic cell placements, not unit inventories.
Resource blobs contrast Northwest wind/solar and Southwest hydro. Monthly resource factors and
CST industrial/evening demand shapes are illustrative, not meteorological observations.

Acceptance tuning: Beijing/Tianjin/Langfang start at 1,500/750/250 MW to keep overnight demand above the inherited thermal fleet’s minimum output. `operating_exclusions.ron` is generated from the shipped India, Southern China and IPS/UPS land rasters (including coastal nudges). It excludes every SGCC cell intersecting a peer land cell, so different grid resolutions cannot reclaim part of an existing operating footprint. This cell-aligned seam is deliberately schematic and can leave a narrow unplayable strip. Regenerate with `python3 -I tools/region_authoring/sgcc_exclusions.py > assets/regions/sgcc/operating_exclusions.ron`. Its header records the input bundle fingerprints.


27 October starter revision (game tuning, not new geographic/economic evidence): new SGCC games
inherit five small independent local grids. Beijing/Tianjin/Langfang retain the original 2,500 MW
combined base load and seven-plant fleet (3,900 MW). Fuzhou, Lanzhou, Changchun and Chengdu each
start at 750 MW base with two existing 500 MW CCGTs and one existing 200 MW peaker: 1,200 MW
maximum and 340 MW minimum per local grid. The resulting authored city base loads total
117,200 MW. Their smaller metro weights are deliberate starter
territories, not claims about observed city demand. Future cities retain their authored weights.
The extra fleets use nearby schematic land cells, share the existing national catalog and are
inherited capital; no new plant tuning, cash rule or real-unit claim is introduced. Each local
fleet covers its own seasonal peak while avoiding excess overnight minimum generation. There
are no starting HVDC links, imports or cross-area AC lines. Archived regions/saves retain their
original starting data.
