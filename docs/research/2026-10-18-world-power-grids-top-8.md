# The world's leading power systems — top eight with the SGCC aggregation exception

## Scope, dates and selection convention

**Research edition: 18 October 2026.** This Equal Earth map shows **eight leading power systems under the stated SGCC aggregation convention**. Capacity primarily refers to **31 December 2025**; operating topology is reviewed separately against information available on **18 October 2026**. This is not a live October 2026 capacity census.

**SGCC is the sole explicit aggregation exception.** Its map color groups five distinct synchronous components: **North–Central China, East China, Northwest China, Northeast China and Southwest China**. North and Central are two administrative regions linked into one AC synchronous component, so this aggregation contains **five physical components across six regional names**. They exchange power through a combination of AC and DC infrastructure; the five components do **not** form one synchronous machine. The remaining seven entries each represent a normally AC-connected main synchronous area, with local and operating-mode qualifications stated below.

**“SGCC system group” is a cartographic grouping of the selected regional networks, not a claim about generation ownership.** In particular, the North–Central component includes western Inner Mongolia on electrical grounds despite its separate corporate operator. The aggregate is not an official SGCC-owned generating-fleet statistic. Southern China's four-province main AC area remains a separate entry; **Yunnan is excluded from both groups and remains unhighlighted**.

The ordering is an estimate, not a certified global generating-unit census. Published inventories differ in date, gross/net ratings, distributed solar, industrial generation, storage and generating-station assignments. The paired IPS/Western positions denote an unresolved order, not an exact tie. Map letters are stable identifiers, separate from estimated rank. **This is a top-eight comparison under an explicit grouping convention, not a ranking of eight wholly synchronous systems.**

## What the colors and capacity mean

A color identifies one selected system. **For A alone, the color spans several synchronous components; for B–H, it identifies a normally AC-connected synchronous area.** A company, country, power pool or market can span several such areas. An HVDC or back-to-back converter can exchange power without joining their frequencies. Sharing nominal 50 or 60 Hz is also insufficient evidence of synchronism. Temporary test configurations and supplier-fed load islands do not establish a permanent national-grid merger.

The intended metric is **active installed generation**, including grid-connected distributed generation and pumped-storage generating ratings. Planned, retired and long-term inactive units are excluded where the source permits; identifiable batteries and flywheels are excluded. A generator need not be producing at the instant of observation to belong to the active fleet. Installed capacity is different from annual generation, demand, dependable output and cross-border transfer capability. Imported electricity or purchased capacity must not be counted again as domestic generation.

The convention cannot be applied perfectly to every source. Approximate labels communicate scale; they do not imply fully reconciled generation-only totals. The SGCC aggregate inherits all component-level inventory qualifications. Grey territory includes other operational grids, isolated systems and sparsely served land. It does not mean that an area lacks electricity. ERCOT, Quebec, Yunnan, the GCC interconnection and regional power pools remain outside the selected highlighting; their omission is not a judgment about their importance.

The source-based sensitivity ranges retained below illustrate inventory and electrical-boundary choices. They are neither statistical confidence intervals nor verified upper and lower bounds.

## A — SGCC system group: the explicit aggregation exception

The map label **approximately 3,170 GW** is obtained from the five regional working estimates below. The subtotal uses rounded and reconstructed inputs; its arithmetic precision is not the precision of an official fleet census.

| Physical synchronous component | Working contribution to aggregate | Basis and qualification |
|---|---:|---|
| North–Central China | ≈1,300 GW | North inferred from a rounded regional ratio plus the four Central provinces |
| East China | ≈700 GW | Published operator total 704.52 GW; battery treatment not fully resolved |
| Northwest China | ≈690 GW | Published regional total 692 GW; scope and battery treatment not fully resolved |
| Northeast China | ≈258 GW | Approximately 258.8 GW provincial/main-Mongdong subtotal; possible generator reassignment |
| Southwest China | ≈220 GW | Provincial/regional generation reconstruction; dedicated-unit allocation remains qualified |
| **Arithmetic subtotal of working contributions** | **3,168 GW** | **Display ≈3,170 GW, or about 3.2 TW** |

The two large source-sensitive contributions retain judgmental generation-only sensitivity ranges of approximately **680–705 GW for East China** and **650–695 GW for Northwest China**. These are not harmonized fleet bounds. The overall SGCC number is therefore a map-scale estimate, not a verified battery-free official corporate statistic. Network transfer capacity and the capacities of imported electricity contracts are not added to it.

### North–Central, East and Northwest components

**North–Central China** combines approximately **824 GW** inferred for North China from an official rounded solar share with approximately **473 GW** across Henan, Hubei, Hunan and Jiangxi. This is an inferred component aggregate, not one official fleet total. The operational **Changzhi–Nanyang 1,000 kV AC link** supports joining North and Central. [NEA's June 2025 technical response](https://www.nea.gov.cn/20250811/5f1ab46a1f3e4adf94751eb196b4e5df/c.html).

The North footprint includes Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shanxi, Shandong and western Inner Mongolia. The northeastern prefectures of Hulunbuir, Xing'an, Tongliao and Chifeng belong to the Northeast geography. Western Inner Mongolia is included on electrical grounds despite its separate corporate operator. Northern Shaanxi generating enclaves connected to North require unit-level assignment; provincial color alone cannot settle them.

**East China** comprises Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Anhui and Fujian. State Grid East reports **704.52 GW** at end-2025, including distributed PV. Its analogous 2024 inventory included storage, so the 2025 number cannot be described as verified battery-free generation. [Operator report hosted by Shanghai government](https://sheitc.sh.gov.cn/djjcdt/20260116/fd180ffebff24242b1000cf3908488f1.html).

**Northwest China** covers the main Shaanxi–Gansu–Ningxia–Qinghai–Xinjiang component. The regional regulator reports **692 GW** at end-2025. Storage conventions and generator enclaves remain unresolved; geographically summing every station in these provinces would not exactly reproduce the synchronous component. [Authored NEA Northwest review](https://www.cpnn.com.cn/news/nytt/202603/t20260307_1871079.html).

These components share one map color under the stated SGCC aggregation exception. That shared color does not change their separate synchronous identities. The detailed Chinese evidence ledgers retain source dates, provincial arithmetic and unresolved inventory differences.

### Northeast component

The working subtotal is **258.8 GW**: Liaoning **91.4**, Heilongjiang **56.1196**, Jilin about **51.4991**, and the Mongdong main grid about **59.7667 GW**. The last two are inferred from official ratios. A conditional deduction of the North-connected Suizhong plant (**3.76 GW**) would produce about **255 GW**, but it must not be subtracted without confirming its inclusion in the source inventory. Storage and dedicated export generators also need reconciliation. [Liaoning issuer filing](https://static.sse.com.cn/bond/bridge2/disclosure/announcement/c/202605/86ca5c_20260509_45UP.pdf); [Heilongjiang annual report](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-23/1225144476.PDF); [Jilin government](https://www.jl.gov.cn/szfzt/jlyhyshjjxs/dxzf/202602/t20260213_3554483.html); [Mongdong operator release](https://inews.nmgnews.com.cn/system/2026/02/05/030305605.shtml).

Do not replace the Mongdong main-grid estimate with all generation geographically in eastern Inner Mongolia. The Russia–Heihe back-to-back link does not merge Northeast with IPS. Shared China–DPRK hydro stations require unit allocation and do not establish that all North Korea belongs to Northeast.

### Southwest component

The main component is **Sichuan–Chongqing–connected Tibet**. A **2025 SGCC-authored paper** explicitly identifies synchronous operation through the Sichuan–Chongqing and Sichuan–Tibet AC links, with asynchronous DC connections to Central and Northwest China. It also classifies approximately **39.1 GW** of nationally dispatched hydro, including Jinguan, Baihetan, Xiangjiaba and Xiluodu, within the Southwest fleet. Export through HVDC is not sufficient grounds to deduct these plants wholesale: remaining AC connections determine their synchronous assignment. [SGCC-authored technical paper, regional network and fleet description](https://www.csee.org.cn/pic/u/cms/www/202505/09141914agtx.pdf); [State Grid regional-network account](https://www.cpnn.com.cn/zt/zt2024/fjqg2024/202409/t20240923_1737913.html).

Approximately **220 GW** is a regional/provincial proxy. Sichuan's listed generation categories sum to **162.5091 GW**, compared with its broader reported **164.6625 GW**; the difference is not automatically identified as battery storage. Chongqing's official 2025 review gives **42.01 GW**, and Tibet's **29 May 2026** official press conference retrospectively confirms **more than 13 GW** at end-2025. The generation-category subtotal is therefore approximately **217.5 GW or slightly higher**, reasonably rounded to **220 GW**. Tibet's observation supersedes the earlier near-year-end forecast. Unit-specific boundary and storage reconciliation remains necessary. [Sichuan issuer report](https://vip.stock.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?CompanyCode=10000481&gather=1&id=12107157); [Chongqing Development and Reform Commission](https://fzggw.cq.gov.cn/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/ghxx/gmjjhshfzgh/202603/t20260313_15527740.html); [Tibet government press conference](https://www.xizang.gov.cn/zwgk/xxfb/xwfbh/xzzzqrmzfxwbgsjxkzqbswwztxwfbjsxzzzqgmjjhshfzswwghgyxgqk/202606/t20260603_543572.html).

## B — Continental Europe–Maghreb

The footprint follows the Continental European synchronous area, including western Denmark (DK1), Türkiye, connected Ukraine and Moldova, the Baltic states, Sicily, Malta and Crete. It excludes the Nordic area, eastern Denmark, Great Britain, Ireland, Sardinia, Corsica and separately operated islands. ENTSO-E membership alone is not the boundary.

Morocco, northern interconnected Algeria and Tunisia belong through the existing Spain–Morocco AC connection. Med-TSO explicitly describes Algerian and Tunisian synchronization with Continental Europe. Isolated southern Algerian systems are excluded; the northern coverage boundary is schematic. [Med-TSO existing interconnections](https://masterplan.med-tso.org/?chapt=4&sect=2); [Enemalta AC interconnector](https://www.enemalta.com.mt/2017/11/16/malta-italy-interconnector/); [ENTSO-E system operations](https://www.entsoe.eu/about/system-operations/).

The preserved country/partial-country ledger sums to **1,356.8 GW**, rounded to **1,350 GW**. Its precise arithmetic is retained for audit only. It combines official observations with dated proxies and explicit assumptions for smaller systems, active Ukrainian plants, distributed solar, islands, rail-frequency generation and cross-border plant allocations. Its estimated position here follows the explicit aggregation convention used for the SGCC entry.

## C — North American Eastern Interconnection

**Eastern:** **893.1025 GW** of U.S. active utility-scale generation + **26.977673 GW AC** of approximately allocated small solar + **62.422846 GW** in connected Canadian provinces = **982.503019 GW**, displayed as **980 GW**. U.S. data are end-2025; Canadian data are end-2024, the latest census used. The EIA filter includes operating units, temporary outages expected to return the next calendar year, and synchronizable standby; it excludes long outages, nonsynchronizable standby, batteries and flywheels. Pumped hydro is already included. [EIA-860](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860/); [EIA-861M](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia861m/index.php); [Statistics Canada](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=2510002201).

The Canadian proxy covers Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia and Prince Edward Island. Quebec, Labrador and Newfoundland are excluded. Provincial totals and reporting-region assignments require small corrections for isolated communities and individual units. For example, Laramie River Unit 1 feeds Eastern while its other units feed Western. [Basin Electric plant description](https://www.basinelectric.com/about-us/Generation/index?location=laramieriverstation).

## D — India, Nepal and Bhutan

India's official **31 December 2025** installed fleet is **513.73 GW**. Nepal and Bhutan add a few GW each, supporting a rounded **520 GW** public-fleet benchmark. Nepal's FY2024/25 report gives **3.591 GW**; Bhutan's current DGPC figure is **3.599 GW**, a later proxy rather than an exact simultaneous census. [Indian government table](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2223720&lang=1&reg=3); [NEA annual report](https://nea.org.np/uploads/shares/publication/77022320.pdf); [Druk Green](https://www.drukgreen.bt/).

Only industrial captive generation that operates in parallel with this AC area should be added. The complete national captive fleet must not be assumed connected. The map therefore displays an explicit unquantified addition rather than a falsely precise total. Mainland India and synchronously connected Nepal/Bhutan are generalized; isolated systems are excluded. Bangladesh's converter interconnections do not justify adding its whole national system, nor does electricity trade justify adding Sri Lanka or Myanmar.

## E — Southern China main area

The main AC component spans **Guangdong, Guangxi, Guizhou and Hainan**, with AC-connected Hong Kong/Macao generation assigned once. **Yunnan has operated asynchronously from this four-province main area since 2016**, connected through DC links. Hainan has existing AC submarine connections. [CSG-authored technical description](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN106786714A/zh); [2016 technical paper](https://iac.iacademic.info/nfdwjs/EN/10.13648/j.cnki.issn1674-0629.2016.07.001); [CSG Hainan AC procurement](https://www.bidding.csg.cn/zbgg/1200433982.jhtml).

The **approximately 500 GW** label reconciles differing provincial and operator scopes only to map-scale precision. Guangdong reports **260.783 GW**, including a combined pumped-hydro/new-storage category; Guizhou reports **105.06 GW** under a broader provincial definition. CSG regional-market reporting uses materially different provincial coverage. Hong Kong's purchased/equity share of mainland Daya Bay must not be added again to Guangdong generation. Local generation and utility tie operating modes also require qualification. [Guangdong Power Exchange report](https://www.jtcopper.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025%E5%B9%BF%E4%B8%9C%E7%94%B5%E5%8A%9B%E8%A1%8C%E4%B8%9A%E5%B8%82%E5%9C%BA%E5%B9%B4%E5%BA%A6%E6%8A%A5%E5%91%8A.pdf); [Guizhou Energy Bureau](https://nyj.guizhou.gov.cn/ztzl/nygg/202603/t20260303_89598053.html).

## F — IPS/UPS synchronous core

The reconstructed ledger totals about **344.5 GW**: Russia's first zone **251.64**, Belarus **13.5**, Kazakhstan **26.807**, Uzbekistan **25.797**, mainland Azerbaijan about **9.6**, Kyrgyzstan **4.14**, main Tajikistan **6.5**, Russia-linked Mongolia about **1.8**, and Georgia about **4.7 GW**. Mixed dates and operating modes justify the map's **340–345 GW** label. The detailed IPS ledger preserves all component sources and deductions. [SO UPS statistics](https://www.so-ups.ru/functioning/ups/ups2026/); [KEGOC annual report](https://ar2025.kegoc.kz/ru/industry-structure.html); [Uzbek government report](https://api-portal.gov.uz/uploads/7/2026/01/27/266f1158-7044-56cc-0b1f-ee2f32147c20_media_.pdf).

**Russia's Far East and Kaliningrad are excluded.** Specialized operator documentation separates the first and second zones; a March 2026 experiment briefly synchronized normally open inter-zone connections and does not establish a permanent merger. Georgia is included with a mode qualification: its joint transmission company documents synchronism with Russia and Azerbaijan, while its contribution explains much of the 340-versus-345 GW sensitivity. [Far East operating description](https://www.so-ups.ru/functioning/ups/oes-east/); [March 2026 switching experiment](https://www.so-ups.ru/odu-east/news/odu-east-news-view/news/29940/); [Sakrusenergo](https://sakrusenergo.ge/en/project/the-switchover-of-the-double-circuit-330-kv-gardabani-1-and-2-transmission-line-at-the-georgia-azerbaijan-border/).

Armenia, Iran, Turkmenistan, Nakhchivan, separately operated Pamir systems and all of Afghanistan are not added from trade, proposed connections or trials alone. Kazakhstan's western and northern/southern regions can belong to the same wider component through Russia even without a direct internal AC link.

## G — North American Western Interconnection

**Western:** WECC reports approximately **337 GW** of resources, including **25 GW of batteries**. Removing batteries and adding roughly **28 GW** of separately reported U.S. small solar gives **about 340 GW**. The addition is supported by the close match between WECC's solar category and EIA's utility-scale solar, but rooftop coverage and border-state allocation remain approximate. [WECC 2026 resource review](https://feature.wecc.org/soti2026/state-of-the-interconnection-2026/resources/index.html).

Western includes western U.S. territory, British Columbia, Alberta and **northern Baja California's interconnected system**. It excludes Baja California Sur, mainland Mexico's main grid, Alaska and isolated northern communities. The map uses generalized operator boundaries rather than treating the Rockies or national borders as exact electrical boundaries.

## H — Brazil's main 60 Hz area

Brazil is the eighth selected system under this map's SGCC aggregation convention and supplies Southern Hemisphere coverage. The label refers to the **main AC synchronous component**, not every generator managed under the SIN umbrella. The regional North, Northeast, Southeast/Centre-West and South divisions do not automatically represent separate synchronous areas. Parallel HVDC corridors also do not split an area that remains connected by AC.

MME reports **259.5 GW nationally** at end-2025 including distributed generation. ONS's December 2025 reference fleet is **248 GW**, including **44.8 GW of distributed generation**. ONS's planning convention excludes some uncontracted thermal plants or periods without a valid variable operating cost; its total is therefore not a complete physical fleet census. **About 250 GW**, with a **240–260 GW scope sensitivity**, is a rounded reconstruction rather than a directly published synchronous-area total. [MME 2025 consolidation](https://www.gov.br/mme/pt-br/assuntos/secretarias/secretaria-nacional-energia-eletrica/publicacoes/boletim-anual-de-monitoramento-do-sistema-eletrico/consolidacao-2025/boletim-especial-consolidacao-2025_snee-ddos-defeso.pdf); [ONS PEN 2026, reference matrix](https://www.ons.org.br/AcervoDigitalDocumentosEPublicacoes/Apresenta%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20resultados%20PEN%202026%20-%20Agentes.pdf); [ONS scope convention](https://www.ons.org.br/paginas/sobre-o-sin/o-sistema-em-numeros).

The **Madeira hydro/export component** requires generator-level deductions. EPE says Jirau normally operates asynchronously; its direct AC transformer is normally disconnected and has been used exceptionally. Six Santo Antônio units connect directly to the Acre/Rondônia AC network, while the principal export arrangement uses conversion. National/SIN capacity cannot be copied unchanged into the main-area total. [EPE Acre/Rondônia planning study](https://www.epe.gov.br/sites-pt/publicacoes-dados-abertos/publicacoes/EstudosPlanejamentoTransmissao/EPE-DEE-RE-057-2024-rev0%20-%20Refor%C3%A7os%20Resili%C3%AAncia%20Acre%20e%20Rond%C3%B4nia.pdf).

**Itaipu's 60 Hz units belong with Brazil; its 50 Hz units do not.** Paraguay, Argentina and Uruguay are not merged merely because Brazil exchanges power with their 50 Hz systems through converters. Imported Itaipu capacity must not be added again. [Itaipu technical information](https://www.itaipu.gov.br/institucional/acesso-informacao); [UTE Melo converter](https://www.ute.com.uy/comunicados/visita-del-ministro-y-el-presidente-de-ute-conversora-de-melo).

Roraima's main system is included following the **September 2025 Manaus–Boa Vista connection**, but Venezuela is excluded. TCU's **29 April 2026 audit**, paragraphs 37–38 and 62–63, states that ONS's 2026 planning contains no Venezuelan imports because that supply arrangement was viable while Roraima was isolated; further integration studies remained pending. The ONS instruction effective **13 May 2026**, sections 4.2–4.3, prohibits simultaneous connection through Manaus and Santa Elena. The numbered audit text, rather than the website's generated overview, supports this conclusion. [TCU Acórdão 1077/2026–Plenário](https://pesquisa.apps.tcu.gov.br/documento/acordao-completo/eletrobras/%2520/DTRELEVANCIA%2520desc%252C%2520NUMACORDAOINT%2520desc/11); [ONS Roraima instruction, revision 24](https://www.ons.org.br/%2FMPO%2FDocumento%20Normativo%2F3.%20Instru%C3%A7%C3%B5es%20de%20Opera%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20-%20SM%205.12%2F3.1.%20Controle%20da%20Transmiss%C3%A3o%2F3.1.1.%20Opera%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Normal%2F3.1.1.4.%20Norte%2FIO-ON.N.5RR_Rev.24.pdf).

## Mongolia: confirmed southern link, unconfirmed full merger

Inner Mongolia's government records **Tavan Tolgoi's 220 kV connection at 00:32 on 16 September 2026**. Russia's operator separately confirms parallel operation with Mongolia's central system in August. These facts do not establish simultaneous continuous Russia–Mongolia–North China synchronism. The project owner describes technical capability; the designer describes broader parallel operation as conditional on further arrangements. [Commissioning report](https://nyj.nmg.gov.cn/ywdt/nydt1/202609/t20260917_2955144.html); [SO UPS statement](https://www.so-ups.ru/odu-siberia/news/odu-siberia-news-view/news/30825/); [project owner](https://www.ot.mn/en/news/зэс-оюу-220-кв-ын-хуваарилах-байгуула); [project-designer interview](https://www.esight.mn/more/61).

The map marks the commissioned southern connection with the SGCC group color, reflecting its North–Central connection and represents **Russia-linked Mongolian systems by a symbol only**. It does not fill the whole country as either component or invent precise service polygons. A joint dispatcher announcement or current operating diagram confirming simultaneous parallel operation would justify regrouping.

## Cartographic method and limitations

**Equal Earth preserves area**, avoiding Mercator's high-latitude area inflation. Natural Earth 1:10 million geography supplies coastlines, national/provincial boundaries and lakes; North American operator boundaries and Inner Mongolian prefectures refine the largest internal divisions. The Chinese mainland grouping combines the five SGCC-associated components under one map color, while Southern China’s main area uses a separate color and Yunnan stays neutral. Component names or boundary cues, where shown, indicate the retained physical distinctions rather than additional legend entries.

Russia's fill uses an **administrative approximation to the first synchronous zone**, not a surveyed electrical service boundary. Russia-linked Mongolian systems are represented by a symbol rather than national shading. Brazil's **whole-mainland fill is a geographic proxy for the main grid's broad coverage**; it does not imply inclusion of every isolated Amazonian community, remote plant or asynchronous generator component. These drawing conventions preserve legibility while the capacity inventory retains the stated physical exclusions.

Apart from the declared SGCC aggregation, color follows geographic approximations to synchronous grid coverage. Political boundaries, connected load areas, generator assignments and switching boundaries are different. Remote islands, isolated communities, generator enclaves, outages and changing operating arrangements remain below world-map resolution. Ukraine's color does not depict military control or imply that every pre-war plant remains active. Base-map disputed boundaries do not express a sovereignty position. An IPS-colored point marks Crimea’s documented electrical connection while retaining the disputed boundary; the wider Ukraine–IPS operating extent is schematic and does not follow complete administrative oblasts. [SO UPS Southern system](https://www.so-ups.ru/functioning/ups/oes-south/).

The artwork does not show proposed supergrids, SINEA projects, pool membership as proof of synchronism, or an invented line mesh. The SGCC exception is a declared presentation convention for existing interconnected components, not a claim that they have recently synchronized. The capacity CSV records dates, scope confidence, exclusions and sources. Earlier European, Chinese, North American and IPS ledgers remain supporting audit material; their extra arithmetic precision should not migrate into display labels.
